# The Mom Test Discovery Interview Script

**Project:** [Project Name]  
**Target Profile:** [Exact user role / community member]  
**Rule:** Never pitch the idea. Never ask "Would you buy X?". Focus 100% on past behavior and active workarounds.

---

## 1. Warm-Up & Context (2 mins)
> *"Hi [First Name], thanks for jumping on. I’m an independent developer researching how people handle [domain/problem]. I’m purely looking to understand how real teams work—I have nothing to pitch or sell today."*

---

## 2. Past Behavior & Workflow (8 mins)
1. *"Can you walk me through the last time you had to [perform task / face problem]?"*
2. *"What tools or steps did you use to get it done from start to finish?"*
3. *"How long did that take you, and who else was involved?"*

---

## 3. Pain Severity & Active Search (5 mins)
4. *"What was the most frustrating part of that process?"*
5. *"Have you actively searched for other software, apps, or templates to fix this? What did you find, and why didn't you stick with it?"*
6. *"In the past 12 months, have you paid money for any tool, course, freelancer, or software in this area?"*

---

## 4. The Commitment Test (Closing 2 mins)
*Choose one commitment ask:*
* **Time:** *"I’m testing a prototype of a 1-click generator next week. Would you be open to a 15-minute call where we run your actual data through it?"*
* **Reputation:** *"Who is the person who feels this headache the most? Would you be comfortable introducing me to them?"*
* **Financial (Smoke Test):** *"We’re launching the first batch to 20 users at an early-bird price of $X. You can lock in your spot today."*
