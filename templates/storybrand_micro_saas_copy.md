# Micro-SaaS StoryBrand Copywriting Framework (SB7 Adapted)

> **Core Axiom:** Customers do not buy the best software. They buy the software they can understand the fastest.  
> **The Golden Rule:** The customer is the HERO. Your micro-SaaS is merely the GUIDE. Never make your tool the hero.

---

## 🎯 The 5-Second "Grunt Test" Hero Section

A visitor landing on your micro-SaaS must be able to answer 3 questions in under 5 seconds before scrolling:

1. **What do you offer?** (Plain English, zero corporate jargon)
2. **How does it make my life better?** (Emotional benefit / time saved / vanity satisfied)
3. **What do I do next?** (One clear, high-contrast Call to Action)

## 📖 The 7 StoryBrand Elements for Micro-SaaS

### 1. A Character (The Customer Desire)
* Identify **ONE** specific desire related to your offering.
* *Example:* "I want to celebrate my football passion / relive the childhood fantasy of going pro."

### 2. Has a Problem (3-Layered)
* **The Villain:** The antagonist (e.g., boring generic birthday gifts, forgotten football memories, routine desk life).
* **External Problem:** "Finding a truly unique, personal gift for a football fanatic is impossible."
* **Internal Problem (How they feel):** "I feel uninspired buying another generic jersey or pair of socks."
* **Philosophical Problem:** "Your birthday shouldn't just be a date on a calendar—it's part of football history."

### 3. Meets a Guide (Your Software)
* **Empathy:** "We've all had that childhood daydream of stepping onto the pitch for our hometown club."
* **Authority / Micro-Credibility:** Powered by 60,000+ historical player records, global geocoding, and archival match databases.

### 4. Who Gives Them a Plan (The Friction-Free 3-Step Process)
Keep the plan strictly to **3 simple steps**:
1. **Step 1 (Input):** Enter your birth date, birth town, and preferred position.
2. **Step 2 (Generate):** Our engine matches your pro twin and builds your alternate career timeline in 15 seconds.
3. **Step 3 (Cherish / Brag):** Share your digital pro card on social or hang a museum-quality framed vintage print on your wall.

### 5. And Calls Them to Action
* **Direct CTA:** "Generate My Pro Dossier" / "Start Free Preview".
* **Transitional CTA:** "View Sample 1994 Matchday Programme".

### 6. That Helps Them Avoid Failure (The Stakes)
* What happens if they don't buy? Another boring birthday with another generic greeting card forgotten in a drawer.

### 7. And Ends in Success (The Vision)
* A framed vintage masterpiece hanging in their office or man-cave that sparks a 30-minute conversation with every mate who walks into the room.

## 📝 High-Converting Landing Page Section Checklist

- [ ] **1. Hero Section:** Grunt test passed; 1 primary CTA; instant visual demo.
- [ ] **2. The Problem / Pain Agitation:** Highlight why existing options are boring or painful.
- [ ] **3. Value Proposition / The Transformation:** Show before vs. after.
- [ ] **4. The 3-Step Plan:** Simple 1-2-3 graphic removing all perceived friction.
- [ ] **5. Social Proof / Community:** Real photo of customer holding the framed print or screenshot of TikTok viral reactions.
- [ ] **6. Pricing / Offer Stack:** Transparent pricing (Free Preview $\rightarrow$ $7.99 High-Res PDF $\rightarrow$ $45 Framed Print).
- [ ] **7. Risk Reversal / Guarantee:** 100% happiness guarantee / instant digital preview before paying.
- [ ] **8. Final CTA & FAQ:** Address top 4 objections (shipping times, small towns, data accuracy).
