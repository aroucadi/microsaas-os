# Opportunity Scoring Rubric

Evaluate each raw idea across these 7 dimensions (Score 1 to 5):

| Dimension | 1 Point (Poor) | 3 Points (Average) | 5 Points (Exceptional) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Hair-on-Fire Pain / Emotional Intensity** | Mild convenience; easily postponed without pain. | Noticeable annoyance; resolved with occasional workarounds. | High urgency (closing deal, legal/tender deadline, or deep emotional love/nostalgia). |
| **2. Time-to-Value (The "Aha!" Moment)** | Requires onboarding call, multi-step migration, or setup > 1 hour. | Generates output in 5–10 minutes after initial signup. | **Instant (Under 60 seconds).** User sees interactive visual preview before paying. |
| **3. Build Simplicity (Solo MVP Feasibility)** | Complex distributed infrastructure, real-time sync, or multi-tenant enterprise auth (> 30 days). | Standard SaaS with database and auth (15–20 days). | **Lightweight engine ($\le 7$ days).** Built with static APIs, Canvas/SVG, or basic serverless function. |
| **4. Operational Passivity** | Requires continuous manual delivery, consulting hours, or ongoing customer support. | Requires 3–5 hours/week of customer support and maintenance. | **100% hands-off.** Automated Stripe checkout and automated digital/POD fulfillment. |
| **5. Willingness to Pay** | Users expect it to be free; heavy resistance to charging > $2. | Standard $5–$15 subscription with typical SaaS churn. | **High conversion.** Impulse $6.99 or high-ticket $49 physical gift bought with zero hesitation. |
| **6. Distribution Accessibility** | Crowded market dominated by $30+ Google Ads CPC and entrenched enterprise sales reps. | Moderate SEO competition; requires ongoing outbound email. | **High organic pull.** Viral short-form video hooks on TikTok/Reels OR programmatic SEO (30k+ long-tail pages). |
| **7. Platform Risk** | Built on an undocumented, private API that can be shut down tomorrow (e.g. unauthorized scraping). | Dependent on one major platform ecosystem (Shopify/Chrome). | **Zero platform risk.** Uses public astronomical math, open-source weather data, or standard public data. |

### Scoring Verdict:
* **$\ge 28$ / 35 Points:** **TIER 1 WINNER** $\rightarrow$ Immediate priority for `microsaas-research`.
* **21–27 Points:** **TIER 2 RESERVE** $\rightarrow$ Viable, but check if an easier wedge exists.
* **$< 21$ Points:** **REJECT** $\rightarrow$ Discard and continue hunting.
